Vision and Image Processing (VIP)

Images and vision, from pixels and gratings to the models that see.

Instructor: Neeraj Kumar Sharma, IIT Guwahati

Modules

  • 01

    Light, eyes and cameras: where an image comes from

    The plenoptic function, the eye, spectra, cones and metamers, photon noise and the Bayer filter.

  • 02

    Images as sums of gratings: the 2-D Fourier transform

    Gratings, the frequency plane, magnitude and phase, windowing and filtering, with live demos on real photos.

  • 03

    Where the eyes go: gaze, reading and visual attention

    Fovea and periphery, reading scanpaths, Yarbus's scenes, visual search, and attention in neural networks.
